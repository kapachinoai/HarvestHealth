Farm & family health advice

How can I help today?

Ask about your crops, animals, or your family's health — in English, Français, Hausa, Yorùbá, or Igbo. You can also send a photo of a leaf, an animal, or the problem.

General advice only. In an emergency, go to a clinic or hospital immediately. HarvestHealth does not replace a doctor, vet, or agricultural officer.
